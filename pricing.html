Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Everything you need to know about how SIGNMARK pricing works.

Yes — every billboard rental package includes artwork design, production, and printing. Our design team will create campaign-ready artwork based on your brand guidelines and brief, at no additional charge. If you have agency-created artwork, we can use that too.

Installation includes professional fitting by our certified team, all hardware and fixings, and removal at the end of the campaign period. Photo proof of installation is sent within 24 hours of display going live.

Campaigns can be cancelled up to 7 days before the start date for a full refund. Within 7 days, 50% of the campaign fee is retained. Once a campaign has gone live, refunds cannot be issued. We'll always work with you to reschedule rather than cancel.

Premium locations — high-traffic highways, city-centre landmarks, major shopping districts — deliver significantly higher impression counts and audience quality. The surcharge reflects higher daily exposure rates and the competitive demand for these specific spots.

Short campaigns (under 1 month): 100% payment upfront before campaign launch. Campaigns of 1 month or longer: 50% deposit on signing, balance due 7 days before live date. Enterprise clients may qualify for 30-day invoice terms.

All prices on this page are shown exclusive of VAT. VAT will be applied at the applicable rate for the campaign location. Formal quotations will clearly show the VAT-inclusive total. We are VAT-registered in all operating jurisdictions.
